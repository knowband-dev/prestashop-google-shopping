# PrestaShop Free Google Shopping Module

PrestaShop Free Google Shopping by Knowband connects a PrestaShop store to Google Merchant Centre, automating the manual job of building and maintaining a product feed. Instead of manually formatting and uploading a product catalogue to Google, store owners can generate a feed directly from their PrestaShop back office and keep it in sync as products, prices, and stock levels change.

Getting listed on Google Shopping puts a store's products in front of shoppers actively searching for them, making it one of the more reliable, cost-effective traffic channels available to e-merchants.

## Features

Key features of the PrestaShop Google Shopping Module include:

- **Easy Listing Process** — Sync or list your entire PrestaShop catalogue to Google Shopping in a few steps.
- **Multiple Feed Support** — Create separate feeds for different countries, languages, and currencies.
- **Exclude Out-of-Stock Products** — Automatically keep unavailable items out of your Google feed.
- **Minimum Price Exclusion** — Choose to exclude products priced below a set threshold in profile management.
- **Exclude Products Without Unique Identifiers** — Automatically skip products missing an EAN13/JAN or UPC code, since Google requires these for most listings.
- **Attribute Management** — Organise attributes like Colour, Size, Gender, and Pattern into groups for cleaner listings.
- **Cron Job Support** — Automatically update and sync your feed with Google on a schedule.

## Installation

1. Go to **Module Manager** and click **Upload a module** to upload the module ZIP file. Once installation is complete, click **Configure** to open the module.
2. You'll be taken to the **General Settings** page, where you can configure the module according to your requirements.
3. Connect the module to your Google Merchant Centre account and configure your feed settings (categories, countries, languages, currencies). See the [user manual](https://www.knowband.com/prestashop-google-shopping) for detailed steps.
4. Set up the cron job for automatic feed synchronisation.
